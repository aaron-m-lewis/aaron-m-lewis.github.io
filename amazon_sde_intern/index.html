Amazon SDE Intern

Date: May 2025 – Aug 2025

Location: Bellevue, WA

During my internship, I restructured the backend of a key recommendation service to significantly improve its scalability. The project was fully customer-facing, with my code deployed to production. To safely roll out the new implementation, I designed and executed an A/B test that routed traffic between the old and new systems, validating correctness and performance at scale. After the new service successfully handled billions of requests without error, I completed the full migration to the updated backend. Following the completion of this project, I also contributed to the creation of a design document for an additional service, supporting future development efforts within the team.
